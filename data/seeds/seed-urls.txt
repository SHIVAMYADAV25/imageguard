# government / science archives. NO adult, "leak", or login-walled sites are listed on purpose:
# adult-site coverage comes from the reverse-image providers, not from our crawler.
# robots.txt is enforced at crawl time: any entry a site disallows is skipped and logged.
# Some URLs may move or 404 over time; the crawler just logs and continues.
# Format: one URL per line, '#' starts a comment.
